Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Quản Lý Lịch Đăng ("the App") is a personal tool operated by natu. This policy explains what information the App uses when you connect a TikTok account.

2. Information the App accesses

With your permission through TikTok Login Kit, the App may access: basic profile information (display name and avatar), a list of your own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares), and your account's follower statistics. The App also stores the videos, captions, hashtags and schedule that you enter into it.

3. How the information is used

This information is used only to show you your own account statistics, to publish the videos you scheduled at the time you chose, and to suggest posting times based on your own results. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage

The App runs on the user's own computer. Access tokens, schedules and statistics are stored locally on that computer. The developer does not operate a server that collects this data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok's official APIs in order to provide the features above.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time by removing its access in your TikTok account settings, and you can delete the locally stored data by deleting the App's data folder. Contact us with any question about your data.

7. Children

The App is not intended for people under 13, and we do not knowingly handle their data.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Contact: namanhtu123@gmail.com.

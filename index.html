Quản Lý Lịch Đăng

A personal video scheduling and analytics tool by natu.

What it is

Quản Lý Lịch Đăng is a personal tool that helps its developer organize, schedule and publish his own original videos to his own TikTok accounts, and review basic performance numbers (views, likes, comments, shares) for those videos.

What it does

The tool runs on the developer's own computer. A user connects a TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and grants permission. The tool can then send videos that the account owner has prepared, together with the caption and hashtags the owner wrote, at the time the owner chose. It also reads the account owner's own video statistics to show which posting times perform best.

What it does not do

It does not follow, like or comment on other users' content, does not collect data about other users, and does not post content the account owner did not create or approve.
